# PRODUCT.md: Threads Marketing Hub & AI Draft Manager

## 1. Product Identity & Purpose
- **Product Name**: Threads Marketing Hub & AI Draft Manager
- **Domain**: Digital Goods & Subscription Commerce (YouTube Premium, Spotify, Netflix, Gemini Pro, Canva, etc.)
- **Primary Goal**: Empower digital product sellers to orchestrate high-converting marketing campaigns on Meta's Threads platform with AI draft generation, interactive natural language revisions, authentic live thread previews, and autonomous Hermes Agent scheduling.
- **Audience**: Digital product store owners, social media marketing managers, solopreneurs.

---

## 2. Core Surface Mode
- **Mode**: `Operate` (App UI / Dashboard / Multi-part Content Editor / Real-time Threads Simulator).
- **Key UX Priorities**:
  1. **Frictionless Review & Edit**: Instant visibility of pending drafts generated by AI.
  2. **Authentic Threads Fidelity**: Live preview renders exactly how Threads looks (avatar, username, connecting thread line, action bar, typography, spacing, 500-char limits).
  3. **Rich Context Management**: Clear structured product catalogs (variants, pricing, USPs, target audience, tone of voice, CTAs).
  4. **AI Copilot Revision Automation**: 1-Click interactive natural language revision assistant right in the thread editor.
  5. **Transparent Hermes Agent Integration**: Real-time status indicators for Hermes API connectivity and Gateway cron executions.

---

## 3. Product Features & Modules

### A. Digital Product Catalog (`/products`)
- Dynamic product management with pricing variants (e.g. 1 Bulan @ 5k, 12 Bulan @ 43k).
- Rich context fields: USPs, Target Audience, Tone of Voice, and Custom CTA Templates.
- Active toggle with tactile accessible switch to control which products feed into autonomous marketing cycles.

### B. Intelligent Draft Review & Interactive Editor (`/drafts` & `/drafts/[id]`)
- **Queue Management**: Status filtering (`PENDING_REVIEW`, `APPROVED`, `SCHEDULED`, `PUBLISHED`, `FAILED`).
- **Multi-Part Post Editor**: Seamless reordering, deletion, and addition of connected thread posts.
- **500 Character Limit Enforcement**: Per-post counter with real-time feedback.
- **✨ AI Copilot Revision**: Natural language prompt input (e.g. *"ubah post 3 jadi ajak DM admin"*, *"bikin gaya FOMO kuota terbatas"*) with targeted part selector and instant preview updates.
- **Authentic Threads Simulator**: Live rendering displaying dynamic store profile (`STORE_NAME`, `@STORE_USERNAME`).

### C. Autonomous Hermes Agent Engine (`scripts/hermes-runner`)
- **Dual Persona Architecture**:
  - **Organic & Tech Knowledge (`productId: null`)**: Senior Software & AI Systems Architect persona focusing on deep technical breakdowns, architecture trade-offs, and tool workflows. Zero forced slang or sales pitches.
  - **Commercial Product Promos (`productId != null`)**: Digital Specialist & Solutions Consultant persona with clear value propositions, transparent pricing variants, official guarantees, and clean CTAs.
- **Balanced Content Strategy**:
  - **Product Promotions**: Highlights product benefits, price advantages, and custom CTAs without cringe marketing slang.
  - **Organic Engagement**: Educational tips, tech insights, and architecture breakdowns without products (`productId: null`).
- **Hermes Gateway Scheduler (`hermes cron`)**:
  - `threads-marketing-post`: Publishes approved drafts every 1 minute.
  - `threads-marketing-generate`: Generates fresh drafts every 2 hours.

### D. Settings & Brand Customization (`/settings`)
- Store Profile (Store Name, Threads Username/Handle, Avatar).
- Hermes API Key management with 1-click token regeneration and copy-to-clipboard.
- Comprehensive API documentation and curl examples for external integrations.
